# OSS Atlas 🗺️

> A living engineering atlas of my open-source contributions, research, experiments, and lessons learned.

**Owner:** [@aspire488](https://github.com/aspire488)

OSS Atlas is the dedicated home for my open-source engineering work. It tracks what I contribute upstream, what I learn from real codebases, and the technical experiments that come out of that work.

This is intentionally separate from **[gh-ops](https://github.com/aspire488/gh-ops)**:
- **gh-ops** → automation, monitoring, intelligence, and OSS discovery.
- **oss-atlas** → the actual contribution record, engineering notes, research, and portfolio.

## 🧭 What lives here

## 📈 Contribution Principles

1. **Understand before changing.**
2. **Prefer small, reviewable patches.**
3. **Test the behavior that changed.**
4. **Respect project conventions and maintainer feedback.**
5. **Document the reasoning, not just the diff.**
6. **Contribute upstream whenever possible.**
